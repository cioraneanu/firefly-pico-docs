---
title: Use Ramble
description: Capture, interpret, review, and create transactions in Pico.
---

## Open Ramble

Select the **Ramble** button from the new transaction screen. The button is available after the language model service has been [configured](/features/ramble/configuration).

## Describe your transactions

Type naturally. You can enter one transaction or describe several in the same message:

```text
Lunch at work 12.50 today, groceries at Lidl 43 yesterday,
and John paid me back 20
```

Include the details you remember. Ramble can understand:

- expenses, income, and transfers;
- several transactions in one message;
- amounts and currencies;
- dates such as “yesterday” or “last Friday”;
- descriptions, accounts, categories, and tags.

You can also use your device's dictation feature. This converts your voice to text on the device, so Pico's transcription service is not required.

## Interpret and review

Select **Interpret** to turn the message into drafts. Open any draft to correct its details, or remove a draft you do not want.

Ramble does not create a Firefly III transaction during this step.

## Create the transactions

When the drafts look correct, select **Create**. Pico creates them one at a time and shows the progress. If a transaction fails, you can correct it and retry without recreating the successful ones.

## Load saved rambles

Messages sent through the [Ramble API](/features/ramble/api) are saved for later. A badge on the Ramble button shows how many are waiting.

Open Ramble and select **Load saved** to add them to the current session. Audio rambles include a playback control. After all resulting transactions are created successfully, Pico removes the loaded rambles from its database.

::note
Always review the drafts. A language model can misunderstand an amount, date, account, or transaction type.
::
