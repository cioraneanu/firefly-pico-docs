---
title: Ramble iOS
description: Capture rambles from your iPhone or Apple Watch with Siri Shortcuts.
---

[Ramble's API endpoint](/features/ramble#the-api-endpoint) makes iOS Shortcuts a perfect companion. You don't even need to unlock your device, just press the "Action button", speak up what you just paid and you're done. 

For convenience there are 2 premade Siri Shortchuts in the repo under [`docs/siri-shortcuts`](https://github.com/cioraneanu/firefly-pico/tree/main/docs/siri-shortcuts) — import one, fill in your URL and token, and you're set. Or build them yourself:

- **text/dictation** flavor: works with only the `ASSISTANT_LLM` configured. It's slightly slower because you have to wait for Apple's on device transcript to resolve your words.
- **voice** flavor: requires `ASSISTANT_TRANSCRIPTION` configured. Is extremely fast and works for languages that Apple doesn't yet support. 

## Use case 1: Dictate → send text

Uses the **Dictate Text** node, so iOS does the speech-to-text and Pico receives plain text. Works without any transcription configuration on the backend.

::steps{level="3"}

### Create the base actions

1. **Text**: your Pico URL, e.g. `https://pico.your-domain.com`
2. **Text**: your authorization header value, e.g. `Bearer your-firefly-token`
3. **Dictate Text**: stop listening *On Tap*

### Send it to Pico

Add **Get Contents of URL**:

- **URL**: the first Text action + `/api/assistant/rambles`
- **Method**: `POST`
- **Headers**: `Accept: application/json`, `Authorization`: the second Text action, `Content-Type: text/plain`
- **Request Body**: `File` → the *Dictated Text*

::

## Use case 2: Record → send audio

Uses the **Record Audio** node and uploads the raw recording. Pico transcribes it server-side, so this needs the `ASSISTANT_TRANSCRIPTION_*` variables configured — in exchange you typically get better accuracy than iOS dictation, especially for mixed-language mumbling, and the recording stays attached to the ramble for playback.

::steps{level="3"}

### Create the base actions

1. **Text**: your Pico URL, e.g. `https://pico.your-domain.com`
2. **Text**: your authorization header value, e.g. `Bearer your-firefly-token`
3. **Record Audio**: finish recording *On Tap*

### Send it to Pico

Add **Get Contents of URL**:

- **URL**: the first Text action + `/api/assistant/rambles`
- **Method**: `POST`
- **Headers**: `Accept: application/json`, `Authorization`: the second Text action
- **Request Body**: `Form`, with one field: type `File`, name `voice`, value the *Recorded Audio*

::

::tip
Either way, the ramble lands in your saved queue — the badge on the Ramble button ticks up, and next time you open Pico you can turn the whole backlog into transactions in one go.
::
