---
title: Ramble iOS
description: Capture rambles from your iPhone or Apple Watch with Siri Shortcuts.
---

[Ramble's API endpoint](/features/ramble#the-api-endpoint) makes iOS Shortcuts a perfect companion. You don't even need to unlock your device, just press the **Action button** , speak up what you just
paid and you're done.


::note
For older models without an Action button you can place the Siri Shortcut on your lockscreen.
::


For convenience there are 2 premade Siri Shortchuts in the repo under [`docs/siri-shortcuts`](https://github.com/cioraneanu/firefly-pico/tree/main/docs/siri-shortcuts) — import one, fill in your URL
and token, and you're set. Or build them yourself:

- **Text / Dictation** flavor: works with only the `ASSISTANT_LLM` configured. It's slightly slower because you have to wait for Apple's on device transcript to resolve your words.
- **Voice** flavor: requires `ASSISTANT_TRANSCRIPTION` configured. Is extremely fast and works for languages that Apple doesn't yet support.

### Nodes to configure in imported Siri shortcut:

1. **Text**: your Pico URL, e.g. `https://pico.your-domain.com`
2. **Text**: your authorization header value, e.g. `Bearer your-firefly-token`
